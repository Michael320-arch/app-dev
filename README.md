
# My Favorite Movie: Interstellar

## About the Movie

**Interstellar** is a science-fiction movie released in *2014*, directed by Christopher Nolan. It follows a group of astronauts who travel through space to find a new habitable planet for humanity.

> "Love is the one thing that transcends time and space."

### Why I Like It

I like this movie because of its:
- Amazing visual effects
- Interesting scientific concepts
- Emotional storyline
- Beautiful musical soundtrack

### Main Characters

1. Cooper
2. Murphy
3. Amelia Brand
4. Professor Brand
5. TARS

### Interesting Fact

The movie explores concepts such as `black holes`, `wormholes`, and `time dilation`.
